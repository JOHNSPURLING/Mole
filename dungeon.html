Dungeon Delve

Your health drains every second. Eat food to survive,
smash the monster spawners, and find the glowing exit.

Move: WASD / Arrow keys  ·  Shoot: Space or J  ·  Potion (clears the screen): B or K  ·  Pause: P
Touch: drag on the left half to move, tap the right half to shoot, two-finger tap for a potion.
Careful: your shots destroy food too!  ·  Back to Whack-a-Mole
